Orders were created, confirmed and moved along through the API where the step was not the one under test; the rating, the hold review and the driver's report were done in the browser.
- **Cause / findings:**
  1. **A delivered order's detail page has no way to rate.** The Orders list sends a delivered order to `/orders/{ref}`, which offers "Download receipt", "Get help" and "Reorder". The rating screen is `/orders/{ref}/delivered`, which only the tracking screen redirects to. A customer who closes the app after delivery cannot find it again from the list. The screen itself works: 4 stars and "Yes, no lifting" saved (`PUT /orders/{ref}/feedback`, 201), and both answers were still selected after a reload.
  2. **The driver app cannot record the unreachable-customer steps.** The backend rule works as written (FR-038): failing an arrived job gave 409 `transition_requirements_unmet` with `missing: ["contact_attempts_recorded", "unreachable_wait_elapsed"]`; after two `POST .../contact-attempts` only `unreachable_wait_elapsed` was missing, and 10 minutes after arrival the same call succeeded: the job ended `failed` with `end_reason: customer_unreachable`. The fork's driver screens have no contact-attempt button and no "customer unreachable" failure. "Customer not reachable" in the problem report sends an `other` incident (`INCIDENT_TYPE` in `features/partner/real/jobs.ts`), which reaches the dispatcher but records no attempt, and the job stays open. The only way to end such a job is the dispatcher's "Fail delivery".
  3. **Small things.** The report screen for an arrived job says "on the way to customer". The three reason options in the dispatcher's "Fail this delivery" sheet have no accessible name (the browser tool listed them as "on"). On a fresh sign-in page, typing the phone and password and pressing Enter without clearing the fields first sent no request on four of the first five tries; clearing the fields first worked every time. This may be the browser tool and not the page.
- **Passed in the browser:** rating (above).
